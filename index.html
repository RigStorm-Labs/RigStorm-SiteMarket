06 / Questions, answered

Before you ask.

How much does a website cost?

Standard websites start from ₹3,500 for suitable lightweight projects (landing pages, showcase sites, portfolios, blogs, small business sites). Advanced projects — e-commerce, CRMs, web apps — are quoted on requirements, starting from ₹15,000.

Do you provide domains?

Domain arrangements depend on the project. A 2nd-level domain arrangement is included per the selected offering; TLD / 1st-level domain registration costs are separate. If you already own a domain, we can work with it.

Can you build an e-commerce website?

Yes. E-commerce with product catalogues, payments, and order flows falls under advanced projects and is quoted according to your specific requirements.

Can you build a CRM or dashboard?

Yes. CRMs, dashboards, booking systems, and other custom web applications are exactly what our advanced track is for. Describe the workflow and we will scope it.

How do I get a quote?

Use the project enquiry form to tell us what you need. We review the requirements and get back to you with the appropriate scope and quote. For complex builds, use the detailed custom quote request.
