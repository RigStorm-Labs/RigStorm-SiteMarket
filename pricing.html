What do 2nd-level and TLD domains mean? Simply: a 2nd-level domain is a website address provided under a shared domain arrangement (lower cost, included), while a TLD / 1st-level domain is your own top-level address like yourbusiness.com (registration cost separate). We'll recommend whichever fits your stage — no DNS lecture required.

Pricing FAQ

Fair questions.

Is everything really included in ₹3,500?

The ₹3,500 starting price covers a professional lightweight website with the 2nd-level domain arrangement per the selected offering. If you want your own TLD (like yourbusiness.com), its registration cost is separate and we'll tell you upfront.

Why isn't there a fixed price for advanced projects?

Because an online store and a CRM are genuinely different builds. A fixed price would either overcharge you or under-deliver. We scope your requirements first, then quote the actual work.

How do I get an exact quote?

Fill in the custom quote request — project type, goals, features, and timeline. We review it and reply with scope and price.

Do you do discounts or urgency offers?

No giant discount banners, no fake countdowns. The entry price is already transparent, and advanced quotes reflect real work.
