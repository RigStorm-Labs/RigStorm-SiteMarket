\# SiteMarket Design System

DESIGN DIRECTION
Modern, bold, sleek, minimalistic, powerful, colourful.
Premium digital studio aesthetic.
Typography-led design with strong visual hierarchy.
Avoid generic AI-agency aesthetics, excessive gradients, glassmorphism,
over-rounded cards, excessive shadows, and template-like layouts.

TYPOGRAPHY

Use for:

* Hero headlines
* H1/H2/H3 headings
* Navigation
* Buttons
* Important numbers

Weights:
600, 700, 800

Secondary — DM Sans
Use for:

* Body text
* Descriptions
* Supporting UI
* Forms
* Secondary navigation

Weights:
400, 500, 600

Accent — Space Mono
Use sparingly for:

* Project labels
* Technical metadata
* Category tags
* Section numbering
* Small UI labels
* Digital/engineering details

Do NOT use Space Mono for large headlines or body text.

COLOUR USAGE

Use charcoal as the dominant background.
Use off-white for primary typography.
Use muted grey for secondary information.
Use electric lime strategically for:

* CTA buttons
* Important words
* Links
* Hover states
* Numbers
* Indicators
* Small graphical accents

Do NOT flood the interface with lime.
The accent should feel intentional and premium.

TYPOGRAPHIC PRINCIPLES

Use large typography confidently.
Use strong contrast between heavy and regular weights.
Do not make every word bold.
Use short, powerful headlines.
Use generous whitespace.
Use tight headline line-height.
Use negative letter-spacing on large headings.
Let typography create much of the visual identity.

Example:

WE BUILD
digital presence.

MOVE BUSINESS.

VISUAL STYLE

Editorial × Digital Studio × Engineering

Prioritize:

* Strong typography
* Large headlines
* Clean grids
* Generous whitespace
* Sharp alignment
* Subtle borders
* Controlled motion
* Strong CTA hierarchy
* Premium minimalism
* Purposeful colour

Avoid:

* Purple/blue AI gradients
* Glassmorphism
* Excessive rounded cards
* Excessive shadows
* Floating random shapes
* Generic SaaS layouts
* Excessive animations
* Stock-photo-heavy sections
* AI-generated visual clutter
* Making everything neon
* Making every section a card

CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The website should feel like a serious digital studio that actually delivers websites.

Typography should communicate:
"These people know what they're doing."

The final result should feel bold, trustworthy, premium, modern,
creative, and established — without looking corporate, generic,
or AI-generated.
