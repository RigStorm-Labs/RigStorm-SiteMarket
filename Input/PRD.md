---

# 1. Product Vision

SiteMarket should not feel like a generic “website maker.”

It should feel like a **professional digital studio that builds websites and digital products for real businesses, creators, organizations, and entrepreneurs.**

The website must communicate:

> **We build digital presence that makes businesses look credible, established, and ready to grow.**

The website itself is the first demonstration of SiteMarket's capability.

If a potential customer lands on SiteMarket, they should quickly understand:

1. What SiteMarket does.
2. Who it is for.
3. What they can buy.
4. How much a basic website starts from.
5. Why they should trust SiteMarket.
6. What actual work SiteMarket has completed.
7. How to start a project.

The site should reduce uncertainty rather than overwhelm the visitor with technical information.
