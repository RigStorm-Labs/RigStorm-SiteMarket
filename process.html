Process / enquiry → launch

Five steps. Zero guesswork.

You bring the business goal in plain language. We handle structure, features, content guidance, design, development, and deployment.

Start a Project
STEP 01 — Tell us what you need

Share your business, goals, and requirements

Use the project form or contact page. No technical vocabulary needed — "I need customers to find me and enquire" is a perfect brief. For advanced builds, tell us the workflow you want (sell products, manage customers, accept bookings).

Step 02 — We plan

Structure, features & approach

We determine the right pages, features, content outline, domain arrangement, and technical approach — then confirm scope and price with you before building.

Step 03 — We build

Design & development

We design and develop the website or application: layout, content integration, functionality, performance, and basic SEO — kept focused, fast, and reviewable.

Step 04 — Review

You review, we adjust

You review the working project and request the adjustments that matter. We refine until it represents your business properly.

Step 05 — Launch

Deploy & hand over

The finished website is deployed and handed over ready to share — with domain, analytics basics, and support contact in place.

What we need from you: your goals, your content (text, logo, photos if you have them), and timely feedback at review stage. What you never need: to understand DNS, frameworks, or hosting — that's our job.
